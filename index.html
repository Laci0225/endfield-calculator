Damage Calculator

Pick an operator, weapon, gear, and the battle conditions. Each ability is calculated from its own multiplier.

Fan-made toolkit for Arknights: Endfield. Not affiliated with Hypergryph or Gryphline.

Skill multipliers are the in-game values also listed on Warfarin. The damage formula, reactions, and status math follow the Endfield Talos Wiki. Character, weapon, and gear numbers are from the 1.6 game tables dated 5 November 2026. Operators released after that snapshot are not in this list yet.
